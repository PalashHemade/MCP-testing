# Database Tables

- [trips](/tables/trips.md) - the central record: one row per shipment
- [customers](/tables/customers.md) - the logistics company's clients
- [vehicles](/tables/vehicles.md) - trucks/trailers owned by the tenant
- [drivers](/tables/drivers.md) - drivers assigned to trips
- [trip_billing](/tables/trip_billing.md) - invoices (multiple per trip allowed)
- [trip_expenses](/tables/trip_expenses.md) - itemized costs per trip
- [logistics_documents](/tables/logistics_documents.md) - policy/port-regulation documents
